Dear Adobe Research Hiring Committee,

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Central Florida, where my research focuses on social reasoning and Theory of Mind in large language models. I am applying for a research internship to study how language and multimodal models represent people, beliefs, and interactions, and to build evaluations that make their strengths and limitations clear.

I am the first author of OmniToM, accepted to the NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations and Datasets Track. In this work, I helped build a human-labeled benchmark and coordinated annotation quality checks, then evaluated API and open-weight models using cloud services and university and NSF ACCESS computing resources. The project gave me experience connecting a research question to a dataset, an evaluation pipeline, and a reproducible analysis. I am interested in bringing this approach to Adobe Research, especially work in natural language processing, intelligent agents, and multimodal systems.

As a Graduate Research Assistant, I also contribute to research proposal development, including IARPA-focused proposals. I recently mentored an REU student on a project to detect specialized language. That work combined translation instability, masked-language-model scores, and other text signals, and examined where the approach did and did not transfer. I would bring Adobe a focused research interest, hands-on model evaluation experience, and a willingness to build the tools needed to answer a research question.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background in social reasoning and model evaluation could contribute to Adobe Research.

Sincerely,\\[0.5em]
