Dear Adobe Research Hiring Committee,

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Central Florida. My primary research interest is social reasoning and Theory of Mind, especially how people reason about others’ beliefs, intentions, and interactions. My current research studies these questions with large language models, and I am open to related research using other methods and modalities.

I led OmniToM, a NeurIPS 2026 paper on Theory of Mind. I worked with collaborators to develop a human-labeled benchmark, coordinated annotation quality review, and evaluated a range of language models. This work taught me to connect a research question with a dataset, a reliable evaluation process, and clear analysis. At Adobe, I am most interested in research related to social reasoning and Theory of Mind, and I am open to different methods and modalities.

As a Graduate Research Assistant, I contribute to research and proposal development, including IARPA-focused proposals. I also mentored an REU student on an NLP project to identify specialized language and evaluate how well the approach generalizes. In addition, I review papers for NeurIPS, AAAI, and ACL. I would bring Adobe a clear research focus, experience building and assessing research systems, and care in communicating what the evidence supports.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my research interests in social reasoning and Theory of Mind, along with my experience in benchmark development and model evaluation, could contribute to Adobe Research.
