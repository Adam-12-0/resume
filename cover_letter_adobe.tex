Dear Adobe Research Hiring Committee,

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Central Florida. My primary research interest is social reasoning and Theory of Mind, especially how people reason about others’ beliefs, intentions, and interactions. My current research studies these questions with large language models, and I am open to related research using other methods and modalities.

I am the sole first author of OmniToM, accepted to the NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations and Datasets Track. In this work, I helped build a human-labeled benchmark and coordinated annotation quality checks, then evaluated API and open-weight models using cloud services and university and NSF ACCESS computing resources. The project gave me experience connecting a research question to a dataset, an evaluation pipeline, and a reproducible analysis. My main interest at Adobe is research related to social reasoning and Theory of Mind, and I am open to different methods and modalities.

As a Graduate Research Assistant, I also contribute to research proposal development, including IARPA-focused proposals. I recently mentored an REU student on a project to detect specialized language. That work combined translation instability, masked-language-model scores, and other text signals, and examined where the approach did and did not transfer. I would bring Adobe a focused research interest, hands-on model evaluation experience, and a willingness to build the tools needed to answer a research question.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my research interests in social reasoning and Theory of Mind, along with my experience in benchmark development and model evaluation, could contribute to Adobe Research.

Sincerely,\\[0.5em]
