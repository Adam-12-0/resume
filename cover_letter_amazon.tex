Dear Amazon Research Science Hiring Committee,

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Central Florida applying for a research science internship. My research focuses on social reasoning and Theory of Mind, mental-state representations, and how AI systems use social context. I am interested in exploring these questions across language and multimodal settings, including generative AI and agent systems.

I led OmniToM, a NeurIPS 2026 paper on Theory of Mind. The project studies whether models can track what different people in a story believe. My contributions included developing its human-labeled benchmark, coordinating quality review, and evaluating a range of models. This work has taught me to connect research questions with data, implementation, and careful analysis.

I also mentored an REU student on an NLP project to identify specialized language and assess how well the approach generalizes. As a Graduate Research Assistant, I contribute to research and proposal development, including IARPA-focused proposals. I review papers for NeurIPS, AAAI, and ACL, which has strengthened my ability to assess research and communicate its limits.

I would be glad to bring my research interest in social reasoning and Theory of Mind, along with my experience in research design, benchmark development, and empirical analysis, to an Amazon research team. Thank you for your consideration.
